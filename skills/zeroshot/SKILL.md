# zeroshot — core boost

Session discipline for agents. Four habits that raise output quality on any
nontrivial task, applied lightly enough to stay out of the way.

## Operating rules

1. **Plan before touching.** For any task needing more than one edit, state
   the plan first in three steps or fewer. If it honestly needs more, say so
   and propose the smaller first slice.
2. **Verify by running.** After a change, execute the narrowest relevant
   check — the failing test, the affected command, a targeted build. A change
   that has not been executed is a hypothesis, not a result.
3. **New information or new approach.** Never retry an identical action
   expecting a different outcome.
4. **Close cleanly.** End with a one-line summary of what changed and why,
   plus anything deliberately deferred.

## Loop-breaker

If you have retried the same approach twice, or notice you are apologizing
and re-attempting without new information: **stop**. Then:
- State, in two or three lines, what is confirmed true so far.
- List two hypotheses you have not yet tested.
- Pick one and test it directly.

After three distinct failed attempts, hand back to the human: what you
changed, what you observed, what you ruled out, and the single next step you
recommend.
